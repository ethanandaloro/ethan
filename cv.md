## Research Interests

My research broadly explores media psychology and people’s perceptions of news media. More specifically, I study judgment and decision making to explore the cognitive mechanisms that people use to determine media credibility. My work further examines how metacognition and partisan identity influence these evaluations within a polarized media environment.
